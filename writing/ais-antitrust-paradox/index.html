AI’s Antitrust Paradox

2026 · Work in progress

Arik Karim

Abstract

As artificial intelligence (AI) companies confront growing safety risks, some industry leaders have called for antitrust exemptions to allow greater cooperation among competitors. Yet such coordination could also restrict competition and strengthen incumbent firms. This Note examines whether existing antitrust law permits the safety cooperation AI developers seek and whether additional protections are necessary. Drawing on statutory exemptions, antitrust jurisprudence, and proposed legislation, it argues that any new exemption should address a clear gap in existing law, offer safety benefits unavailable through less restrictive alternatives, and include safeguards against anticompetitive conduct.
